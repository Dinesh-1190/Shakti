Our Story

Built on Strength & Trust

Founded in 1995 by Ex-Army personnel Sangram Kishor Das, Shakti Security Agency was established with a clear mission: to provide businesses and institutions in Odisha with reliable, professional, and affordable security solutions rooted in military discipline and integrity. Starting from humble beginnings in Angul, we have grown into a full-service security and facility management company.

Today, we serve clients across Angul, Dhenkanal, Sambalpur, and beyond — deploying over 500 trained security personnel and managing facilities for hospitals, industries, apartments, and corporate offices.

Our Journey

Three Decades of Excellence

From a small security unit to a full-service agency — here's how Shakti Security Agency has grown over the years.

1995
Foundation
Shakti Security Agency was established in Angul, Odisha by Ex-Army personnel Sangram Kishor Das with a team of 10 trained security guards serving local businesses.
2003
Expansion Across Odisha
Expanded operations to Dhenkanal and Sambalpur districts, growing our guard deployment to over 100 personnel.
2010
PSARA License Obtained
Became fully licensed under the PSARA Act, strengthening our legal authority to operate security services across Odisha.
2016
CCTV Division Launched
Started our CCTV installation and surveillance services division to offer complete security technology solutions.
2019
Facility Management Wing
Expanded into full facility management services for hospitals, industrial zones, and residential complexes.
2024
500+ Guards, 200+ Clients
Today we proudly serve 200+ clients with 500+ deployed personnel across multiple districts in Odisha.
