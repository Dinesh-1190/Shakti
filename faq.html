01. General Questions

What services does Shakti Security Agency provide?

+

We offer comprehensive security and facility management solutions including:

  • Armed & Unarmed Security Guards for residential, commercial, and industrial properties
  • CCTV Installation & Monitoring with 24/7 surveillance
  • Facility Management including housekeeping, maintenance, and pest control
  • Manpower Outsourcing for businesses and institutions
  • Event Security for corporate events, weddings, and public gatherings
  • Cash & Valuables Transportation with armed escort

Where do you operate?

+

We are headquartered in Angul, Odisha, and serve clients across Angul, Dhenkanal, Sambalpur, Rourkela, Bhubaneswar, and surrounding districts. We can deploy security personnel and install CCTV systems anywhere in Odisha within 24-48 hours.

Are you licensed and registered?

+

Yes. Shakti Security Agency is fully licensed under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 (PSARA) and registered with all necessary government authorities. All our security guards are verified, trained, and deployed in compliance with state and central regulations.

How experienced is your team?

+

With over 31 years of experience since 1995, we have successfully served hospitals, industries, corporate offices, residential complexes, and government institutions. Founded by Ex-Army personnel Sangram Kishor Das, our team includes ex-servicemen, trained security professionals, and certified facility managers.

03. Security Personnel

How are your security guards trained?

+

All our guards undergo rigorous training including:

  • 80+ hours of basic security training (PSARA-compliant)
  • Fire safety and emergency response protocols
  • First aid and medical emergency handling
  • Access control and visitor management systems
  • Conflict resolution and customer service
  • Site-specific training based on client requirements

We also conduct quarterly refresher training and performance evaluations.

Are background checks conducted on all guards?

+

Absolutely. Every security personnel undergoes:

  • Police verification and criminal background checks
  • Address verification and reference checks
  • Document authentication (Aadhaar, PAN, educational certificates)
  • Medical fitness examination

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for any past criminal activity.

Can I request a replacement if I'm unhappy with a guard?

+

Yes. Client satisfaction is our priority. If you are dissatisfied with any personnel for any reason, we will provide a replacement within 24-48 hours at no additional cost. Simply notify our operations team, and we'll take immediate action.

What is the difference between armed and unarmed guards?

+

Unarmed Guards: Ideal for residential complexes, offices, retail stores, and low-risk environments. They handle access control, visitor management, and routine surveillance.

Armed Guards: Required for high-security locations like banks, jewelry stores, cash transport, factories with valuable inventory, and high-risk events. They are licensed to carry firearms and trained in threat neutralization.

How long is CCTV footage stored?

+

Storage duration depends on the DVR/NVR hard disk capacity and camera resolution:

  • Standard Setup: 15-30 days of continuous recording
  • Extended Setup: 60-90 days with larger storage drives
  • Cloud Backup: Unlimited storage with subscription plans

We help you choose the right storage solution based on your compliance and security needs.

+

Yes. Our AMC packages include:

  • Quarterly system health checks and camera cleaning
  • Software updates and security patches
  • Priority support for technical issues
  • Free replacement of faulty cables and connectors
  • Discounted rates on camera upgrades and repairs
